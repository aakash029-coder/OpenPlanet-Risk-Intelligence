# Privacy Policy — OpenPlanet

**Last updated: October 29, 2026**

## 1. Core Climate Risk Analysis

OpenPlanet allows users to explore climate risks and projections without creating an account. The core analysis uses geographic coordinates, such as latitude and longitude, to generate climate risk information.

These coordinates are used to provide location-specific results. The core analysis does not require users to provide their names, email addresses, or other identifying information.

## 2. Google Sign-In (Optional)

Users may choose to sign in with Google if authentication is enabled in the deployment. When they do, the application may receive their name, email address, and authentication information through the sign-in provider.

This information is used to support authentication and maintain the user's session. The application does not require sign-in to access the core climate risk analysis.

## 3. Analytics and Cookies

Google Analytics may be enabled by the site operator to understand how visitors use the platform.

When analytics is enabled, it loads only after the user gives consent through the consent interface. If the user declines, Google Analytics should not load.

Google Analytics may process information such as cookie identifiers, device or browser information, and IP addresses, subject to Google's processing practices and configuration.

Analytics can be disabled by the site operator. Users can also decline analytics through the consent interface.

## 4. Contact Form

If a user submits the contact form, the information they provide, such as their name, email address, and message, is sent to the application's backend so that the team can respond.

Contact information is used for communication and support. It is not sold to third parties.

## 5. Local Storage

OpenPlanet may use the browser's local storage to retain application preferences, scenario selections, and the user's analytics consent choice.

This information helps the application maintain relevant settings between interactions. Users can clear locally stored information through their browser settings, although doing so may reset application preferences.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Depending on the deployment configuration, OpenPlanet may communicate with external services to retrieve climate data, load map tiles, retrieve fonts, provide authentication, or support analytics.

These services may receive technical information, including IP addresses, as part of normal network communication. Their handling of this information is governed by their respective privacy policies and practices.

OpenPlanet aims to keep external integrations configurable so that operators can choose alternative or self-hosted services where supported.

## 7. Data Retention

Contact form submissions should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request and address related follow-up.

Authentication information may remain available for the duration of the relevant session and any additional period required by the application's actual session configuration.

Analytics retention depends on the analytics configuration and the policies of the relevant provider.

The platform's retention practices should be reviewed whenever the deployment configuration or data storage mechanisms change.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion and, where applicable, to withdraw consent.

To submit a privacy request, contact the OpenPlanet team through the project's official GitHub repository:

https://github.com/aakash029-coder/openplanet-climate

Please avoid including passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive information in public issues.

Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and the data the project actually holds.

## 9. Privacy and Legal Compliance

OpenPlanet aims to follow privacy-by-design principles, including data minimization, transparency, and limiting the collection of personal information to what is necessary for the relevant functionality.

The project considers applicable privacy requirements, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), where applicable.

Publishing this policy does not, by itself, establish legal compliance. Actual compliance depends on the platform's implementation, deployment configuration, data handling practices, and applicable legal obligations.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the platform's functionality, integrations, or data handling practices change. The latest version will be published in this repository, together with an updated revision date where appropriate.
