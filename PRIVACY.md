# Privacy Policy - OpenPlanet

- **No PII Collection:** We do not collect or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, emails, or phone numbers unless you explicitly submit it via the contact form.
- **Data Usage:** Location coordinates (Latitude/Longitude) are used solely for real-time climate risk processing and are not linked to individual users.
- **Local Storage:** We use localStorage only for essential session state (e.g., scenario recall). No third-party cookies are set by default.
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics is **disabled by default**. If `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID` is configured, analytics are only loaded **after explicit user consent** via the cookie/consent banner. You can decline analytics at any time; consent state is stored locally.
- **Third-Party Requests:** Map tiles, fonts, and climate data APIs may be contacted as part of normal operation. See deployment docs for self-hosting options.
- **Contact Form:** If you submit the contact form, the data you provide is transmitted to our configured backend/contact delivery endpoint for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. We do not sell submitted contact data.
- **Compliance:** We follow the principles of data minimization in accordance with global privacy standards.
