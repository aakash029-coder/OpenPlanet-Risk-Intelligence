# Privacy Policy - OpenPlanet

Last updated: [DATE]

## What the core analysis uses
Searching for a city and viewing its climate risk projections does not require an account. We use only the location (latitude and longitude) for real-time climate risk processing, and it is not linked to an identity.

## Sign-in (optional)
If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We use them only to maintain your session. [If you store the email in a database, change this line to say what is stored and why.]

## Analytics
Google Analytics is loaded only if the site operator has configured it, and only after you give explicit consent through the consent banner. If you decline, it is not loaded. Analytics can process online identifiers such as cookie IDs and IP addresses. Your consent choice is stored locally in your browser.

## Contact form
If you submit the contact form, the name, email address and message you provide are sent to our backend so that we can reply to you. We do not sell this data.

## Local storage
We use your browser's localStorage only for essential state such as your scenario selections and your consent choice.

## Third-party requests
Map tiles, fonts and climate data APIs may be contacted as part of normal operation, and these services may receive your IP address. See the deployment docs for self-hosting options.

We rely on your consent as the legal basis for processing, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and GDPR for users in the EU. You can withdraw consent at any time.
